MASTER CAMPUS SPECIFICATIONS & ARCHITECTURAL SUMMARY

1. Site Sizing: 1.49 Acres (64,904 SF) on the 900 block of NE Jefferson Ave, Peoria, IL 61603 (380' frontage × 170' depth). Natural 14-foot grade drop from NE Jefferson sidewalk (El. 514') downhill to the rear alley (El. 500').

2. Revised 9-Level Program: Lower 2 (Subgrade Basement) + Lower 1 (Banked Walkout: parking & delivery bay) + Level 1 (Street Concourse: Bodega & Coffeeshop) + Level 2 (All-Gender / Family / Executive Deck) + Deck 3 (Full-Width Thermal Spa with Central Glass Roof Atrium Pool) + Decks 4–7 (Twin Gender-Separated Towers: West Men's, East Women's) + Twin Independent Rooftop Sky Decks above Deck 7.

3. Concentric Floor Architecture: 100% of sleeping pods sit on the exterior perimeter building walls, ensuring every guest enjoys a switchable PDLC Smart-Glass Mirror-Window. 100% of private bathrooms sit in the center core separated by a 6-foot continuous acoustic circulation hallway.

4. Total Capacity: 336 Total Sleeping Pods (144 Men's, 144 Women's, 48 All-Gender/Family) + 184 Private Full Bathroom Suites (Pod Type B) + 105 Parking Stalls.

5. Central Core Cargo Lift & Panelized Swap Logistics: The central core integrates an industrial 10,000 lb (ASME A17.1 Class C-3) freight elevator (10'×12' cab, 8' bi-parting doors) connecting Lower 1 delivery dock to all levels. Every sleeping pod is engineered as 5'×10' panelized cassettes (wall, floor, ceiling) allowing any suite to be replaced, upgraded, or reconfigured within 4 to 6 hours via wheeled A-frame carts down the 6' concourse without exterior cranes or building structural modification.
