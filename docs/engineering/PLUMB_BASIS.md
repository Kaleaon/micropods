### Strategic Civil & Engineering Benefits:
1. **Zero Off-Site Main Extension Costs:** No street-tearing or expensive municipal water/sewer main extensions required. Utilizing the five distinct taps saves ~$180,000–$250,000 in municipal impact fees, street repaving bonds, and off-site infrastructure permits.
2. **Elimination of Massive Internal Collector Mains:** Standard large facilities require 8"–10" internal gravity sewer collector pipes sloped across hundreds of feet of floor space (consuming valuable ceiling clearance). Spreading the load across **five independent 6" sanitary sewer outfalls** allows direct, short, downward gravity drops into the municipal collector with zero complex pumping or massive horizontal pitch runs.
3. **Dedicated Load Segregation:**
   * High-surge commercial uses (laundry plant discharge, pool backwash, commercial dishwashers) do **not** share piping with residential guest pods, eliminating pressure fluctuations, thermal shocks, or sewer gas back-siphonage.
4. **N+1 Manifold Redundancy in Lower 3:**
   * All 5 water supply risers enter the **Lower 3 Heavy MEP Vault** through RPZ backflow preventers and tie into a central emergency cross-connection header. If the City of Peoria shuts off any single main for street repairs, motorized isolation valves automatically cross-feed the affected wing from adjacent services.

## 3. Water Heating & Domestic Distribution Architecture

### Central Domestic Hot Water (DHW) Plant (Lower 3)
* **Heat Pump Technology:** Closed-loop commercial heat pump water heaters (R-1234ze eco-refrigerant) with natural gas condensing backup boilers in Room B3-160.
* **Storage Capacity:** Two (2) 1,250-gallon insulated glass-lined ASME storage tanks (**2,500 gallons total** at 140°F).
* **Legionella & Scald Mitigation:**
  - Water is generated and stored at **140°F (60°C)** to completely sterilize Legionella bacteria.
  - Dual thermostatic master mixing valves (ASSE 1017) blend distribution down to **120°F (49°C)** for core bathrooms and **110°F** for public lavatories.
  - Dedicated 140°F direct loop to Lower 2 Commercial Laundry Plant.
* **Recirculation:** Continuous dual variable-speed bronze Grundfos circulator pumps maintaining hot water within 3 seconds of any core bathroom fixture.

## 5. Sleeping Pod Micro-Plumbing Basis (Pod Types A, C, D, E)

No toilets or full showers exist inside sleeping pods. Sleeping pods only feature a localized water dispenser/air quality station (`SPEC-KLN-WD-2026`):

* **Supply:** 1/2" flexible PEX-A line connected via quick-disconnect push-fit brass shutoff valve.
* **Water Column:** Integrated instant flash-heating (185°F espresso/tea) + Peltier thermoelectric cooling (42°F chilled sparkling/still water) + ultrasonic level sensor.
* **Drainage:** 1/2" indirect drain line connected to a localized 1.5" trap arm with an atmospheric air-gap (IPC § 802).
* **Leak Protection:** Sealed stainless steel base pan with ultrasonic moisture sensor and **250ms automatic motorized emergency shut-off solenoid valve**.
