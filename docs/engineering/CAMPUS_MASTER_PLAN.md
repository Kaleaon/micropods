## 1. Overview & Conceptual Architecture

The 900 NE Jefferson campus is engineered as a **9-level banked hillside development** on a 1.49-acre parcel (380' frontage × 170' depth) with a 14-foot natural grade fall toward the Illinois River.

Based on program optimization, the building is organized into:
1. **The Operational & Logistics Base (Lower 2 & Lower 1):** Subgrade laundry/freezers and low-side walkout parking/delivery bay.
2. **The Street Concourse (Level 1):** Public Bodega, Coffeeshop, and Member Reception.
3. **The All-Gender / Family Floor (Level 2):** Couples, families, executive double pods, and universal ADA suites.
4. **The Grand Spa & Thermal Deck (Deck 3):** Spans the entire building footprint under a **soaring central glass roof**, housing the Men's Sento (West), Women's Sanctuary Spa (East), and Central Glass Atrium Pool.
5. **The Twin Residential Towers (Decks 4–7):** Four dedicated single-gender sleeping levels in each tower (West: Men's Tower; East: Women's Tower).
6. **Twin Dedicated Rooftop Terraces (Above Deck 7):** Separate open-air sky decks for men (West) and women (East).

## 3. Architectural Highlights of the Revised Flow

### A. The Buffer Logic of Level 2 (Family & Gender-Neutral)
Placing the **All-Gender and Family Pods on Level 2** directly above the Level 1 concourse creates an intuitive transition:
* Couples traveling together, visiting physicians, families with children, or guests who prefer not to stay on single-gender floors have direct, immediate elevator access from the lobby without passing through restricted-gender zones.
* Provides a quiet acoustic buffer between the bustling street-level grocery/coffeeshop and the thermal spa deck above.

### B. Deck 3: The Grand Thermal Span & Central Glass Atrium
By extending **Deck 3 across the full width of the building** under a central glass atrium roof:
* Natural daylight floods into the center of the building, creating a breathtaking indoor/outdoor atmosphere.
* The West Wing houses the **Men's Sento & Bathhouse**, the East Wing houses the **Women's Sanctuary Spa**, and the center houses the **Glass-Roofed Heated Lap Pool and Solarium**, allowing shared or scheduled pool enjoyment with complete gender privacy in the bathhouses.
* Structurally, Deck 3 acts as the **transfer podium deck** for the two residential towers rising above it.

### C. Decks 4 through 7: High-Privacy Sleeping Sanctuaries
* **West Tower (Men's Residential):** Four levels (Decks 4–7) of keycard-secured, quiet sleeping pods and private bathrooms.
* **East Tower (Women's Residential):** Four levels (Decks 4–7) of keycard-secured, quiet sleeping pods and private bathrooms.
* **Acoustic Separation:** Tower residents are elevated far above street noise, enjoying silent, conditioned, circadian-lit sleeping micro-suites.

### D. Twin Private Rooftops (Above Deck 7)
Each gender receives their own private, open-air rooftop sanctuary overlooking the Illinois River:
* **West Rooftop (Men's Sky Deck):** Open-air natural gas fire pits, stargazing daybeds, weather-protected calisthenics & stretching deck.
* **East Rooftop (Women's Sky Deck):** Private sunbathing cabanas, outdoor yoga/pilates turf, sensory botanical garden, and shaded relaxation daybeds.

## 5. Central Core Heavy-Duty Cargo Lift & Rapid Pod Swap Infrastructure

To provide 100% operational lifecycle agility and future-proof the campus against obsolescence, the central core houses an industrial-rated freight and pod rigging elevator:

* **Hoistway Location:** Central Service Core, directly adjoining the main Lower 1 delivery loading dock (El. 500.0') and the central MEP vertical shafts.
* **Hoistway Dimensions:** **12'-0" Width × 14'-0" Depth** masonry shaft.
* **Elevator Platform (Clear Cab):** **10'-0" Width × 12'-0" Depth × 10'-6" Height** clear interior.
* **Rated Capacity:** **10,000 lbs [4,535 kg]** (ASME A17.1 Class C-3 Heavy Material Handling Freight).
* **Door Configuration:** Vertical bi-parting counterbalanced steel freight doors (**8'-0" Width × 9'-0" Clear Height**).
* **Operational Swap Capabilities:**
  1. **Fully Assembled Wet Core Transport:** Sized to hoist complete 10'×10'×10' Pod Type B bathroom modules (which arrive plumbed and tested).
  2. **Panelized Cassette Transport:** Accommodates heavy wheeled A-frame transport carts bearing flat-pack 5'×10' cold-formed steel wall, floor, and ceiling cassettes.
  3. **Continuous Occupancy Swap:** Any guest suite can be removed, upgraded, or reconfigured (e.g. converting a standard twin Pod Type A into two executive solo Pod Type C suites) via the cargo lift in **4 to 6 hours** without disrupting adjacent pods or requiring cranes.
