## 1. Property Overview, Civil Features & Infrastructure Advantages

The **900 NE Jefferson Ave** campus is engineered as a **10-level hillside urban resort and multi-family micro-pod development** on a prime 1.49-acre parcel (64,904 SF) with a natural 14-foot grade fall toward the Illinois River:

### Key Parcel Attributes:
* **Zoning:** **R-6 Multi-Family High Density Residential** — directly supports multi-family residential density; mixed-use commercial (bodega/café) authorized under Planned Unit Development (PUD) overlay.
* **Frontage:** **Full Block of Continuous NE Jefferson Avenue Frontage (380'-0")** × 170'-0" depth to rear alley (El. 500.0'). Provides uninterrupted street-level retail presence, prominent architecture, and multiple pedestrian portals.
* **Site Condition:** **Cleared Lots Ready for Immediate Construction** — 100% shovel-ready with zero structural demolition, asbestos abatement, or raze permit delays.
* **Municipal Utilities:** **Five (5) Water Supplies & Five (5) Sewer Supplies** existing along the property frontage and rear alley, enabling direct zoning load distribution (Residential West, Residential East, Commercial Laundry/BOH, Spa/Pool, and Retail/Fire Service) without costly off-site municipal main upgrades.
* **Prime Strategic Location:** Located directly on major Peoria transit corridors, walking distance to Downtown Peoria and the Riverfront, and only **0.58 miles from OSF Saint Francis Medical Center** (the largest regional medical complex and employer in downstate Illinois).

### 10-Level Building Organization:
1. **The Subterranean Vaults (Lower 3, Lower 2 & Lower 1):**
   - **Lower 3 (El. 480.0'):** Deep subgrade mission-critical server room / data center (MDF), 3000A switchgear, 250 kW generator, and 45,000-gal stormwater detention vault.
   - **Lower 2 (El. 490.0'):** Staff recharge breakroom w/ commercial kitchenette & 4 nap pods, staff locker rooms w/ 4 private showers, commercial laundry plant, and wholesale bodega freezers.
   - **Lower 1 (El. 500.0'):** Banked walkout 65-stall covered EV parking garage and commercial loading dock with 10,000 lb Cargo Lift landing.
2. **The Street Concourse (Level 1 - El. 514.0'):** Public Bodega (7,000 SF), Specialty Coffeeshop (3,000 SF), and Hotel Lobby (8,000 SF).
3. **The All-Gender / Family Floor (Level 2 - El. 530.0'):** Couples, families, executive double pods, and 16 universal ADA accessible suites.
4. **The Grand Spa & Thermal Deck (Deck 3 - El. 544.0'):** Spans the entire building footprint under a **soaring central glass roof**, housing the Men's Sento (West), Women's Sanctuary Spa (East), and Central Glass Atrium Pool.
5. **The Twin Residential Towers (Decks 4–7 - El. 559.0' to 599.0'):** Four dedicated sleeping levels in each tower (West: Men's Tower; East: Women's Tower) with perimeter pods and central core bathrooms.
6. **Twin Dedicated Rooftop Terraces (Above Deck 7 - El. 599.0'):** Separate open-air sky decks for men (West: fire pits, calisthenics) and women (East: cabanas, yoga lawn).

## 3. Architectural Highlights of the Revised Flow

### A. The Buffer Logic of Level 2 (Family & Gender-Neutral)
Placing the **All-Gender and Family Pods on Level 2** directly above the Level 1 concourse creates an intuitive transition:
* Couples traveling together, visiting physicians, families with children, or guests who prefer not to stay on single-gender floors have direct, immediate elevator access from the lobby without passing through restricted-gender zones.
* Provides a quiet acoustic buffer between the bustling street-level grocery/coffeeshop and the thermal spa deck above.

### B. Deck 3: The Grand Thermal Span & Central Glass Atrium
By extending **Deck 3 across the full width of the building** under a central glass atrium roof:
* Natural daylight floods into the center of the building, creating a breathtaking indoor/outdoor atmosphere.
* The West Wing houses the **Men's Sento & Bathhouse**, the East Wing houses the **Women's Sanctuary Spa**, and the center houses the **Glass-Roofed Heated Lap Pool and Solarium**, allowing shared or scheduled pool enjoyment with complete gender privacy in the bathhouses.
* Structurally, Deck 3 acts as the **transfer podium deck** for the two residential towers rising above it.

### C. Decks 4 through 7: High-Privacy Sleeping Sanctuaries
* **West Tower (Men's Residential):** Four levels (Decks 4–7) of keycard-secured, quiet sleeping pods and private bathrooms.
* **East Tower (Women's Residential):** Four levels (Decks 4–7) of keycard-secured, quiet sleeping pods and private bathrooms.
* **Acoustic Separation:** Tower residents are elevated far above street noise, enjoying silent, conditioned, circadian-lit sleeping micro-suites.

### D. Twin Private Rooftops (Above Deck 7)
Each gender receives their own private, open-air rooftop sanctuary overlooking the Illinois River:
* **West Rooftop (Men's Sky Deck):** Open-air natural gas fire pits, stargazing daybeds, weather-protected calisthenics & stretching deck.
* **East Rooftop (Women's Sky Deck):** Private sunbathing cabanas, outdoor yoga/pilates turf, sensory botanical garden, and shaded relaxation daybeds.

## 5. Twin Heavy-Duty Cargo Lifts & Rapid Pod Swap Infrastructure (North & South Towers)

To provide 100% operational lifecycle agility and future-proof the campus against obsolescence, each tower incorporates an industrial-rated freight and pod rigging elevator:

* **Dual Hoistway Architecture:**
  * **North Tower Cargo Lift (CL-1):** Located in the North Tower core, serving Lower 3 through Deck 7 / North Rooftop. Opens directly to the North commercial loading bay on Lower 1.
  * **South Tower Cargo Lift (CL-2):** Located in the South Tower core, serving Lower 3 through Deck 7 / South Rooftop. Opens directly to the South commercial loading bay on Lower 1.
* **Hoistway Dimensions (Each):** **12'-0" Width × 14'-0" Depth** reinforced concrete/masonry shaft.
* **Elevator Platform (Clear Cab):** **10'-0" Width × 12'-0" Depth × 10'-6" Height** clear interior per cab.
* **Rated Capacity:** **10,000 lbs [4,535 kg] per lift** (ASME A17.1 Class C-3 Heavy Material Handling Freight).
* **Door Configuration:** Vertical bi-parting counterbalanced steel freight doors (**8'-0" Width × 9'-0" Clear Height**).
* **Operational Swap & Logistics Capabilities:**
  1. **Zero Central Atrium Cross-Traffic:** Heavy pod cassettes, laundry hampers, and commercial deliveries travel vertically inside their own tower core, completely avoiding the central Deck 3 Grand Spa atrium and pool deck.
  2. **Fully Assembled Wet Core Transport:** Sized to hoist complete 10'×10'×10' Pod Type B bathroom modules.
  3. **Panelized Cassette Transport:** Accommodates heavy wheeled A-frame transport carts bearing flat-pack 5'×10' cold-formed steel wall, floor, and ceiling cassettes.
  4. **Continuous Occupancy Swap:** Any guest suite can be removed, upgraded, or reconfigured (e.g. converting a standard twin Pod Type A into two executive solo Pod Type C suites) via the respective tower's cargo lift in **4 to 6 hours** without disrupting adjacent suites or requiring exterior mobile cranes.
  5. **Operational Redundancy:** If one freight elevator is scheduled for preventative maintenance or occupied with suite turnover, the opposing tower operates at 100% freight and laundry capacity.
