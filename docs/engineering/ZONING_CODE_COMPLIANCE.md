### PUD Entitlement Strategy (City of Peoria Planning Commission)
1. **Planned Unit Development (PUD) Overlay:** Petition for PUD zoning overlay across the 1.49-acre R-6 parcel to authorize the integrated mixed-use program (Mercantile Bodega + Assembly Café/Spa + Residential Pods).
2. **Height Variance Justification:**
   * Natural 14-foot topography masks the two lower levels entirely on the uphill NE Jefferson Ave elevation (only 7 stories appear above grade).
   * Contextual alignment: Located just 0.58 miles from OSF Saint Francis Medical Center complex (which features 8-to-12 story clinical towers).
   * Public amenity tradeoff: 7,000 SF fresh produce grocery store serving a designated Peoria food-access improvement corridor, plus public sidewalk café and civic landscaped plaza.
3. **Parking Variance & Transit Reduction:**
   * Standard suburban code requires 1 space per dwelling unit (~336 spaces).
   * Peoria UDC § 8.3 permits up to a 60% parking reduction for transit-oriented, medical-adjacent micro-lodging with shared mobility:
     - 65 covered keycard parking stalls (w/ 20 Level 2 EV chargers and 4 DC Fast chargers).
     - 40 designated surface / valet stalls in rear commercial apron.
     - 60-bike secured indoor bicycle parking facility in Lower 2.
     - 0.58 mi walking distance to OSF Medical District and CityLink bus lines along Jefferson & Adams.

### IBC 510.2 "Podium" Building Separation Justification:
* **Podium Deck:** Lower 2, Lower 1, Level 1, Level 2, and Deck 3 are engineered as a **Type I-B / I-A concrete and protected structural steel podium** with a minimum **3-hour horizontal fire-resistance rating** at the transfer level.
* **Twin Towers Above Podium:** Decks 4 through 7 (rising 4 stories above Deck 3) are treated as separate residential structures under IBC 510.2, allowing rapid, highly precise modular panelized construction while remaining well within statutory height and area tables.

## 3. Illinois Accessibility Code (IAC) & ADA Title III Compliance

1. **Continuous Accessible Path of Travel:**
   * Street Grade (NE Jefferson Ave): Zero-step grade-level entrance at El. 514.0' with 1:20 gentle threshold slopes to the Bodega, Coffeeshop, and Lobby.
   * Sidewalk Plaza: Exterior ADA ramp with 1:12 maximum slope, 36" clear width, continuous 34"-38" dual handrails, and level 60"×60" landings every 30 feet.
   * Parking to Concourse: Direct elevator access from Lower 1 accessible parking stalls (4 ADA van-accessible stalls with 8' access aisles) directly into Level 1 and all residential decks.
2. **Accessible Pods (Pod Type F — Universal Accessible Suite):**
   * Per ADA Standards § 224: A 336-bed facility requires a minimum of **14 fully accessible rooms (5%)** with roll-in showers.
   * We allocate **16 Pod Type F Universal Accessible Suites** (15'×10' footprint) on Level 2:
     - 60" clear turning diameter inside room.
     - 32" minimum clear door opening with lever handles (< 5 lbf opening force).
     - Centrally paired with dedicated **Pod Type B-ADA roll-in shower bathroom suites** in the central core directly opposite the concourse.
3. **Vertical Elevators & Stretcher Compliance (IBC § 3002.4):**
   * All passenger elevators accommodate a standard 24" × 84" ambulance stretcher in the horizontal open position.
   * **Central Core Cargo Lift (10'W × 12'D cab)** exceeds all stretcher criteria and accommodates standard pallet jacks, wheeled rigging carts, and hospital gurneys.

## 5. Thermal Pest Remediation (140°F) Fire & Mechanical Exemption Basis

The campus implements a building-wide thermal bed-bug protocol (holding pods at 140°F for 3 hours between guest rotations). To ensure full life-safety compliance without triggering false alarms or damaging MEP infrastructure:

1. **Fire Sprinkler Head Rating (NFPA 13 § 7.2.4.2):**
   * Standard ordinary sprinkler heads actuate at 135°–170°F (which would rupture during 140°F cycles).
   * **Mandatory Specification:** All residential pod sprinkler heads are specified as **Intermediate Temperature Rated (212°F / 100°C - Green Bulb)**.
2. **Plumbing Seal Exemption:**
   * Wax toilet rings melt at 125°–130°F.
   * **Mandatory Specification:** All water closets use **Fernco / HydroSeat wax-free elastomeric silicone seals rated to 180°F+**.
3. **Mechanical HVAC Negative-Pressure Balancing:**
   * Each pod is exhausted via an ERV run at **50 CFM continuous** with motorized fire/smoke dampers. During a 140°F heat cycle, supply air is boosted while return air dampers modulate to maintain a **slight negative pressure (-5 Pa)** relative to the 6-foot concourse, preventing thermal escape into the hallway.

## 6. Geotechnical & Hillside Slope Protection (Peoria Stormwater Code)

The site drops **14 feet** from NE Jefferson Ave down to the rear alley:

1. **Foundation Retaining Wall:**
   * 12" cast-in-place reinforced concrete foundation retaining wall along the uphill (NE Jefferson) side.
   * Perimeter foundation drain tile with perforated HDPE piping connected to a dual-pump sump basin in Lower 2.
2. **Earth Retention & Shoring:**
   * Secant pile / soldier pile and lagging shoring system during excavation to protect the NE Jefferson Ave sidewalk and municipal utility mains (12" water main, 24" combined sewer).
3. **Stormwater Detention (Peoria UDC § 8.5):**
   * To prevent runoff surge into the Peoria combined sewer system during 100-year storm events:
     - 45,000-gallon underground concrete stormwater detention vault located beneath the Lower 2 foundation slab.
     - Hydrodynamic vortex oil/grit separator treating all parking deck runoff before discharge.
