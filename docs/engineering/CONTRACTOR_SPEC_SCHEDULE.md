## 5. Division 21 & 22: Fire Suppression & Plumbing Master Matrix

1. **NFPA 13 Fire Sprinklers:**
   - **Pod Interiors:** Concealed flat-plate quick-response sprinklers rated at **212°F (100°C) intermediate temperature**, preventing accidental discharge during 140°F bed-bug heat remediation treatments.
   - **Data Center (Lower 3):** Clean-agent gaseous fire suppression (**Novec 1230 / FK-5-1-12**), 10-second total flooding discharge, zero water, zero residue, zero electrical conductivity.
2. **Plumbing Fixtures (Division 22):**
   - **Water Closets:** Floor-mounted commercial vitreous china, dual-flush (1.1 / 1.6 GPF), installed with **Fernco silicone wax-free gaskets (rated to 200°F)** to eliminate heat degradation.
   - **Lavatories & Sinks:** Solid-surface integrated bowls, laminar flow commercial faucets with 0.5 GPM WaterSense aerators.
   - **Showers:** Solid-surface walls, 36"×42" walk-in footprint, linear stainless trench drains with easy-clean hair strainers, thermostatic pressure-balanced mixing valves.

## 6. General Contractor Delivery & Phasing Guidelines

1. **Pre-Construction Civil (Month 1–3):**
   - Site is 100% cleared and shovel-ready. Mass excavation for Lower 3 and Lower 2 (14' slope grade cut).
   - Tie into the 5 NE Jefferson water mains and 5 rear alley sanitary sewer outfalls simultaneously.
2. **Concrete Podium & Core Slipform (Month 4–7):**
   - Cast post-tensioned concrete slabs for Lower 3 through Deck 3.
   - Pour reinforced concrete central shear core and elevator hoistway shafts.
3. **Twin Tower Steel & Modular Shell (Month 8–11):**
   - Erect structural steel frame for Twin Towers (Decks 4–7) and roof trusses for the central glass atrium.
4. **Pod Cassette Logistics & Rigging (Month 12–14):**
   - Off-site manufactured panelized cassettes arrive via Lower 1 commercial loading bay.
   - Hoisted via the 10,000 lb Cargo Lift to assigned floors; assembled in 4 to 6 hours per suite using cam-lock structural connections and quick-connect MEP harnesses.
5. **Commissioning & Turnkey Handover (Month 15–16):**
   - Balance HVAC DOAS, test DALI-2 circadian lighting curves, verify Novec 1230 envelope hold time, and complete City of Peoria Certificate of Occupancy (CO) inspections.
