## 1. The 5-Foot Universal Modular Grid (10', 15', 20')

To ensure all modules **snap together seamlessly** with zero wasted space, uniform column grids, and standardized factory fabrication, all pod types are strictly engineered on a **5'-0" exterior dimensional increment**:

### Master Exterior Envelopes:
* **Height:** **10'-0" [3048 mm]** exterior across all modules.
* **Depth:** **10'-0" [3048 mm]** exterior across all modules.
* **Widths (Multiples of 5'-0"):**
  * **10'-0" Module [3048 mm]** = **1.0 Grid Unit** (100 SF Footprint)
  * **15'-0" Module [4572 mm]** = **1.5 Grid Units** (150 SF Footprint)
  * **20'-0" Module [6096 mm]** = **2.0 Grid Units** (200 SF Footprint)

```
====================================================================================================
THE 5-FOOT UNIVERSAL SNAP GRID (10'D x 10'H Constant)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[10' x 10' MODULE]                [15' x 10' MODULE]                   [20' x 10' MODULE]
(1.0 Grid Unit / 100 SF)          (1.5 Grid Units / 150 SF)            (2.0 Grid Units / 200 SF)
- Pod Type B (Twin Full Bath)     - Pod Type D (Couples Suite)         - Pod Type A (Twin Solo Standard)
- Pod Type C (Executive Solo)     - Pod Type F (Universal ADA Suite)   - Pod Type E (Family Quad Suite)
- Pod Type B-ADA (Single Bath)                                         - Pod Type B2 (Quad Full Bath)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INTERCHANGEABILITY EQUATIONS:
  * Two 10' Modules   = One 20' Module   (20'-0" run)
  * Two 15' Modules   = Three 10' Modules = 30'-0" run
  * Four 15' Modules  = Three 20' Modules = 60'-0" structural bay
====================================================================================================
```

### Wall Thickness & Clear Interior Dimensions:
* **Wall Assembly:** Exterior envelope uses 3-1/2" structural multi-layer plywood/LVL with fire-retardant core and acoustic mineral wool insulation.
* **Clear Interior Offsets:**
  * **10' Exterior:** Clear Interior = **9'-5" [2870 mm]** width × **9'-5"** depth × **9'-5"** ceiling.
  * **15' Exterior:** Clear Interior = **14'-5" [4394 mm]** width × **9'-5"** depth × **9'-5"** ceiling.
  * **20' Exterior:** Clear Interior = **19'-5" [5918 mm]** width × **9'-5"** depth × **9'-5"** ceiling.

## 3. Floor Grid Alignment: Why the 5-Foot Snap Works

```
====================================================================================================
CORRIDOR SNAP ALIGNMENT (60-FOOT STRUCTURAL COLUMN BAY)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PERIMETER SLEEPING WALL (OUTSIDE):
[ POD A (20') ] + [ POD D (15') ] + [ POD F (15') ] + [ POD C (10') ] = 60'-0" CLEAR
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
========================= 6'-0" CONTINUOUS CIRCULATION HALLWAY =====================================
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CENTER BATHROOM CORE (INSIDE):
[ BATH B2 (20') ] + [ BATH B (10') ] + [ ELEVATOR/STAIR (20') ] + [ BATH B (10') ] = 60'-0" CLEAR
====================================================================================================
```

By standardizing every module to **10', 15', or 20' exterior widths** on a constant 10' depth and height:
1. Modules line up on opposite sides of the 6-foot hallway with clean, perpendicular alignment.
2. Standard structural steel column grids (20' × 20' or 30' × 30') fit the pods without odd furring strips or dead space.
3. Modules can be swapped or reconfigured during factory prefabrication without altering the building’s structural or MEP rough-in layout.

## 4. Central Core Heavy-Duty Cargo Lift & Rigging Hoistway

To ensure any pod module or sub-assembly can be delivered, installed, or swapped out during building occupancy without requiring exterior mobile cranes, the central core integrates a dedicated heavy-duty freight elevator:

* **Hoistway Footprint:** **12'-0" Width × 14'-0" Depth** reinforced concrete masonry unit (CMU) / structural steel shaft.
* **Elevator Platform (Clear Cab):** **10'-0" Width × 12'-0" Depth × 10'-6" Interior Height**.
* **Rated Capacity:** **10,000 lbs [4,535 kg]** — ASME A17.1 Class C-3 Heavy Industrial / Material Handling Freight Rating.
* **Hoist Type:** Heavy gearless traction (or direct-acting multi-stage telescopic hydraulic piston) operating at 100 fpm with micro-leveling.
* **Entrance Doors:** Counterbalanced vertical bi-parting steel freight doors with wire-glass vision panels, providing **8'-0" Width × 9'-0" Height clear opening**.
* **Direct Logistics Alignment:**
  * **Lower 1 Delivery Dock:** The lift opens directly to the enclosed 14-foot clearance commercial truck loading bay (Lower 1, El. 500.0').
  * **Upper Decks (Levels 2 through 7):** The lift opens directly into the 6'-0" wide concourse circulation spine and central MEP service chase.
* **Cargo Load Clearances:**
  * Accommodates an assembled **Pod Type B (10'×10'×10') Twin Bathroom module**.
  * Accommodates heavy wheeled A-frame rigging carts loaded with **5'×10' and 10'×10' flat-pack wall, floor, and ceiling cassettes**.

## 5. Panelized Modular Prefabrication (Cassette Sub-Assemblies)

Every sleeping pod (Types A, C, D, E, F) is designed using a **segmented cassette prefabrication system** so that all structural components can be transported via the central cargo lift and maneuvered through the 6-foot concourse:

```
====================================================================================================
PANELIZED CASSETTE EXPLODED COMPONENT BREAKDOWN (5-FOOT INCREMENTS)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ROOF / CEILING CASSETTES ]   5'x10' pre-finished acoustic panels, recessed LED channels, ERV boots
          ^
          | (Quick-connect cam-locks + neoprene acoustic vibration seals)
          v
[ PERIMETER WALL CASSETTES ]   5'x10' cold-formed steel (CFS) framing, mineral wool, pre-wired conduit
          ^
          | (Pre-hung 3'x7' acoustic doors + 4'x7.2' PDLC smart mirror-window pre-installed)
          v
[ FLOOR BASE CHASSIS ]         5'x10' perimeter steel skids, integrated leveling jacks, subfloor decking
          ^
          | (Rolls on rubber swivel casters or air-caster skids directly into position)
          v
[ UTILITY MEP SPINE ]          Plug-and-play quick-disconnect Wago harnesses + push-fit PEX supply lines
====================================================================================================
```

### Key Engineering Features of the Panelized System:
1. **Dimensional Segment Limits:** No individual rigid panel exceeds **5'-0" Width × 10'-0" Length × 6" Depth** (maximum weight: 260 lbs per wall cassette; easily handled by 2 technicians).
2. **Structural Cam-Locks:** Panels lock together with heavy-duty internal rotary cam-locks (accessible via discreet interior trim plugs). No hot-work, welding, or structural fasteners required on the residential floors.
3. **Acoustic Decoupling (STC 55+):** Every panel-to-panel joint features dual-bulb EPDM/silicone acoustic gaskets and staggered stud cavity offsets, preventing flanking transmission.
4. **Quick-Disconnect Utility Harnesses:**
   * **Electrical/Data:** Twist-lock Wieland/Wago connectors for 120V circuits, 240V thermal heater lines, and Cat6 PoE feeds.
   * **Plumbing (Water Bar):** Push-fit Viega ProPress / SharkBite flexible PEX-A lines with 250ms automatic shutoff solenoid valves.
   * **HVAC:** Insulated flex duct quick-clamp collars connecting into the central core corridor supply plenum.
5. **Rapid Pod Swap-Out Workflow (4 to 6 Hours Total):**
   * **Step 1 (Disconnect - 30 min):** Unplug quick-disconnect electrical/data harnesses and isolate PEX supply valves.
   * **Step 2 (De-Cam - 60 min):** Release cam-locks on ceiling and wall cassettes using standard hex keys.
   * **Step 3 (Transport - 45 min):** Load panel cassettes onto wheeled A-frame rigging carts; roll down 6' concourse to the Central Cargo Lift; lower to Lower 1 dock.
   * **Step 4 (Replacement Assembly - 120 min):** Wheel replacement pre-finished cassettes (e.g. converting a Pod Type A into two Pod Type C suites, or installing a newly renovated unit) into the bay and cam-lock into the floor chassis.
   * **Step 5 (Commissioning - 30 min):** Plug in MEP harnesses, initiate digital keycard lock pairing, and certify air balancing.
