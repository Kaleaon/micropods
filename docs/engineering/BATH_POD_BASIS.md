# Bath Pod Module — Engineering Basis & Scope
**Pod Type B (Full Bathroom) | 10'W × 10'D × 10'H Modular Wet Core**  
**Date:** 2026-10-16 | **Status:** Preliminary Engineering & Architectural Specification  

---

## 1. Overview & Concept of Operation

The **Pod Type B** is a 10'W × 10'D × 10'H standalone modular wet unit designed to seamlessly cluster with **Pod Type A** (20'W × 10'D × 10'H Twin Sleeping Pods). 

To maximize guest privacy, operational efficiency, and ROI in a $50/night micro-hotel, the 10'×10' envelope is engineered in two configurations:

### Configuration 1: "Twin-Suite" Split Bath (BOD / Standard)
- Divides the 10'×10' footprint into **two fully independent, private 5'W × 10'D full bathrooms** (Bath B1 and Bath B2), separated by a sound-insulated central chase partition.
- **1:1 Pairing Ratio:** One 10'×10' Bath Module pairs directly with one 20'×10' Type A Twin Pod (Pod A assigned private keycard access to Bath B1; Pod B assigned private access to Bath B2).
- Each private bathroom contains:
  1. **Walk-in Shower:** 36" × 42" with solid-surface surround, low-threshold acrylic/cast resin pan, thermostatic pressure-balancing valve (2.0 GPM), linear trench drain.
  2. **Water Closet:** Commercial elongated toilet (dual-flush 1.1 / 1.6 GPF, wall-hung or floor-mounted with wax-free silicone gasket).
  3. **Vanity:** 24" wide solid surface vanity top with undermount porcelain basin, single-lever sensor/lever faucet, backlit LED anti-fog mirror.
  4. **Amenities:** Radiant cove heater, towel warmer bars, touchless soap/shampoo dispensers, robe hooks.

### Configuration 2: Universal Accessible Suite (ADA / ICC A117.1)
- Single open 10'×10' full wet-room for accessible units:
  - 60" turning diameter wheelchair circle.
  - Roll-in barrier-free shower (36"×60") with fold-down padded bench and grab bars.
  - ADA-compliant 17"–19" rim height toilet with code-required grab bars and flush handle placement.
  - 34" max rim height roll-under vanity sink with insulated supply/drain piping.

- **DWV Main:** 4" DWV stack located in rear 18" service chase. Connects directly to building sanitary sewer trunk.
- **Slope:** 1/4" per foot minimum fall.
- **Venting:** 2" dry vent riser looping up through module roof or tied into building central vent stack.

- **Option A (Central Building Loop - Preferred):** 3/4" hot supply drop with recirculating line from building central boiler.
- **Option B (Self-Contained Module):** 240V / 30A on-demand tankless electric water heater (e.g. Stiebel Eltron Tempra 24 Plus) or 50-gallon high-efficiency hybrid heat pump water heater mounted in the rear utility chase.

## 3. The 140°F Thermal Remediation Protocol in the Wet Core

Bed-bug thermal remediation cycles (140°F for 3 hours) impose unique constraints on plumbing and wet fixtures:

1. **Wax Ring Prohibition:** Standard beeswax toilet seals melt between 125°F and 135°F. Squeezing melted wax causes sewer gas leaks and water damage. **Requirement: Must use wax-free elastomeric synthetic rubber/silicone seals** (e.g., Fernco Wax-Free Toilet Seal or Danco HydroSeat), rated continuously to 180°F+.
2. **Piping & Traps:** All supply lines must be **PEX-A (cross-linked polyethylene)** rated to 200°F @ 100 psi. Drain lines must be schedule 40 PVC/ABS or high-temp polypropylene; P-traps must use solvent-welded joints rather than slip-joint friction washers that loosen under thermal expansion.
3. **Wall Linings:** High-pressure laminate or solid surface panels (Swanstone / Corian / Wilsonart Wetwall) installed with high-temperature neutral-cure silicone adhesives (rated to 300°F). No peel-and-stick vinyl tiles.

## 4. Mechanical Ventilation & Moisture Control

- **Code Minimum:** IMC / ASHRAE 62.2 requires 50 CFM intermittent or 20 CFM continuous per bathroom.
- **Design Specification:** 
  - Dual 80 CFM ultra-quiet EC inline exhaust fans (e.g., Panasonic WhisperSense DC) with integrated condensation/humidity sensors.
  - Sound rating: < 0.3 Sones (silent operation).
  - Interlock damper: Motorized spring-return backdraft damper that closes tight during pod thermal remediation mode to prevent hot air escaping through exhaust ducts.
